# Pitch Deals

Section 03 of five. Slides 19 to 21. Core question: what transactions or partnerships shape the
thesis?

## Triggers

- The hub reaches Phase 4, Key Deals, after Competition.
- A request about a specific acquisition, partnership, or capital transaction and whether it matters.

## Inputs

- Deal filings, press releases, and company investor materials, with announcement dates.
- The Competition section, since a deal often changes the peer picture.

## Required elements

- The transactions and partnerships that matter most to the thesis.
- For each one: the target, the consideration, the strategic rationale, and what it changes about the
  story.

Detail: `references/deals.md`.

## Analyst owns

Which deals are thesis-relevant and which are noise. A deal can be large and still irrelevant to the
case, and that call is the analyst's.

## Evidence

The tag shape is in `sourcing-standard`. Terms come from the filing or the press release, with the
announcement date recorded.

## Output

A sourced Key Deals section draft, mapped to slides 19 to 21, ending with the judgment calls the
analyst still owns.

## Pitfalls

- **A deal list instead of a deal argument.** Enumerating transactions is not a section. Each entry
  says what it changes about the story, or it does not belong.
- **Consideration stated without its form.** Cash, stock, and assumption of debt are different costs.
- **Undated deals.** A three-year-old acquisition read as recent misleads the reader about the
  current shape of the company.
