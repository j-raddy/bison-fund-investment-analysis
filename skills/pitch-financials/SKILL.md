# Pitch Financials

Section 04 of five. Slides 22 to 28. Core question: does the money work, revenue, margins, returns,
cash?

## Triggers

- The hub reaches Phase 4, Financials, after Key Deals.
- A request about the revenue trend, margins, returns, or cash generation.

## Inputs

- The filed statements, not third-party summaries.
- The fact sheet, so chart values and slide values come from one place.

## Required elements

- Revenue trajectory with compound annual or year over year growth, and the named drivers.
- Unit economics and revenue per user: what each customer is worth, and the cost to acquire them.
- Return metrics: return on assets, return on equity, return on invested capital, with peak callouts.
- Margin structure over time: gross, operating, EBITDA, net.
- Liquidity and balance sheet strength: current and quick ratios, cash, undrawn credit.
- Cash flows: cash from operations, capital expenditure, free cash flow.

Detail: `references/financials.md`.

## Analyst owns

Which financial trend is the one that actually drives the valuation. Several trends will be visible;
naming the load-bearing one is the analyst's call.

## Evidence

The tag shape is in `sourcing-standard`. This section is chart-led: a chart per slide with a Key
takeaway, and the chart values match the fact sheet exactly, because the review pass checks them
against it.

## Output

A sourced Financials section draft, mapped to slides 22 to 28, chart-led, ending with the judgment
calls the analyst still owns.

## Pitfalls

- **A margin definition that changes between slides.** Gross margin on one slide and adjusted gross
  margin on the next reads as a trend that does not exist. Fix the definition for the section.
- **Growth presented without its base.** A percentage without the period it covers is not a fact.
- **Charts that do not match the fact sheet.** The chart audit exists because this happens.
- **Aggregating non-GAAP figures into a trend line** and labeling the result as reported.
