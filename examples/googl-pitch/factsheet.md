## Phase 1 intake (analyst: Jacob Radke, 2026-10-09)
- Thesis: vertical stack. Alphabet is cloud provider, model provider, and application provider. Benefits from the AI buildout from both directions: sells the compute rivals need (including the open-source wave) and fields its own frontier models. [opinion]
- Market view: stock is roughly fairly priced, possibly a little rich. Not deeply misunderstood. [opinion]
- Right condition: frontier labs and open-source keep renting Google Cloud; Google's own frontier models stay competitive because of the vertical stack. [opinion]
- Kill condition: Microsoft's internal tools win the agent landscape, or a neocloud or AWS takes the compute share. Cloud revenue and earnings growth is the crux. [opinion]
- Prior: BUY. [opinion]
