02 Competition

The competitive field

Who competes for what

Cloud: Amazon Web Services and Microsoft Azure, plus the neoclouds. Models: OpenAI and Anthropic at the frontier, open weights everywhere. Agents: Microsoft's Copilot stack inside enterprise distribution. Search: Answer engines and assistant surfaces carving queries away from the results page.

Where Alphabet stands

The only competitor holding positions in all three layers at once, and the only one whose model competitors are also its customers. Microsoft holds two layers and rents the third from OpenAI. Amazon holds one and buys the rest.

Key takeaway

The kill condition is specific: Microsoft winning the enterprise agent frame, or a neocloud or Amazon Web Services taking the compute share. Cloud revenue growth of 82% and a $514B backlog say that has not happened yet.

05 Valuation

The bull case

1. The backlog is contracted revenue

$514B converting within 24 months, at margins that just went from 20.7% to 35.6%. The model's free cash flow ramp may still be conservative.

2. TPU systems is a new product line

Selling the machine changes the compute layer from rental margin to hardware plus rental margin, the Amazon Web Services to Alphabet's own infrastructure.

3. Distribution nobody can copy

A billion monthly users of AI Mode, 90% of the Fortune 100 on the enterprise platform, and the default search box. Distribution is the hedge if models commoditize.

4. The option portfolio is free

Waymo, Isomorphic, and the $87.1B equity portfolio sit outside the model. Optionality the market is not paying for, per the model's own math.

The bull case rests on the sourced figures above. The model value of $314 assumes none of the four land above their current run rates.

05 Valuation

The bear case and the risks

Backlog repricing

Weight: medium. Compute commitments are priced at signing. If capacity loosens, renewals reprice down, and the 35.6% margin is the peak of the cycle.

Microsoft's agent frame

Weight: medium. If enterprise agent workflows consolidate into the Microsoft 365 stack, Gemini Enterprise's penetration has to convert against Redmond's distribution advantage.

Search monetization under AI Mode

Weight: medium. The cash cow funds $200B a year of capex. Artificial intelligence answers compress clicks; management reports incremental queries, the counter is lower revenue per query.

Open weights running elsewhere

Weight: noise to low. Open weights run on any cloud, but the default surfaces and the enterprise contracts still route through Google.

Dilution from the June raise

Weight: low to medium. $18B of common stock plus $16.75B of mandatory convertible preferred (6.25%, converting by May 2029) plus a $40B at-the-market program. The share count grows into the model's per-share value.

Securities class action, filed October 9, 2026

Weight: low for the model, but it is the news of the week. The suit alleges misrepresentation of Gemini 3.5 Pro progress after a 4% stock drop. It is disclosure risk, not operating risk.

Key takeaway

The analyst ruled the net-cash-evaporation case low weight: $126.8B of cash against a $200B year is deliberate, and the equity portfolio is a store of value, not a liability. The risks that matter are the two contract-priced ones: backlog repricing and the agent frame.
