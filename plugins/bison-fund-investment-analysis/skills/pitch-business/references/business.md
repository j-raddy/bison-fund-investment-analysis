# Section 01, Business, Products and Industry Outlook

Slides 01 to 10. Core question: what is this business, and is its market growing?

## Required elements

- One-line company description and headline scale metrics (revenue, customers, assets).
- Products and services, segment by segment, each with its revenue contribution where disclosed.
- Platform or operating KPIs the market actually trades on (users, net deposits, assets under custody).
- Industry outlook and the structural drivers (wealth transfer, mobile-first behavior, fee compression).
- Market opportunity: total and serviceable addressable market sizing with a cited source and a stated
  compound growth rate.
- Geographic and product footprint.

## Analyst owns

The narrative of why this business is durable, not just what it sells. The assistant assembles the
record; the durability claim is the analyst's.

## Evidence

Every figure carries the tag shape from `sourcing-standard`. The section's Key takeaway is the
analyst's take on what the record says, never a standalone opinion.
