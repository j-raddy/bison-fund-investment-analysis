# Section 02, Competition, M&A and Leadership

Slides 11 to 18. Core question: who does it compete with, and who runs it?

## Required elements

- A peer comparison table on consistent metrics: net revenue, growth, net income, margin, accounts,
  client assets, revenue per account, market capitalization, price to earnings.
- Revenue diversification: how the mix shifts across periods, and what the shift means.
- The regulatory landscape that shapes the business model.
- The competitive moat, evidenced. Recurring subscription revenue against volume-dependent revenue is
  the kind of distinction that carries evidence.
- Recent M&A and the rationale for each deal.
- Management team: tenure, background, executive compensation against peers.

## Analyst owns

Whether the moat is real, and whether management is aligned. The assistant lays out the comparison;
the judgment is the analyst's.

## Evidence

Every figure carries the tag shape from `sourcing-standard`. Peer metrics come from each peer's own
filings or an established data provider, never from an aggregator page.
