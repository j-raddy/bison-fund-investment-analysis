# Section 03, Key Deals

Slides 19 to 21. Core question: what transactions or partnerships shape the thesis?

## Required elements

- The transactions and partnerships that matter most to the thesis.
- For each one: the target, the consideration, the strategic rationale, and what it changes about
  the story.

## Analyst owns

Which deals are thesis-relevant and which are noise. A deal can be large and still irrelevant to
the case, and that call is the analyst's.

## Evidence

Every figure carries the tag shape from `sourcing-standard`. Deal terms come from the filing, the
press release, or company investor materials, with the announcement date recorded.
